### Reading
pc1's last-handshake timestamp (1788747516) is identical before and after
forced pings that failed. pc1 attempted to re-handshake and got no response —
handshake sent, never answered. UDP 51820 is dropped at gateway_router in
both directions, so the tunnel is dead while the underlay path (20.0.0.2)
stays reachable. Matches the original hand-test signature exactly, and is
the same pc1-side symptom as the overlay half (pair 3a) — the difference is
only visible at the gateway_router iptables layer.
